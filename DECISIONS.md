# Decisions

Dated, irreversible-leaning decisions, one entry each, newest last. The
reasoning lives in `PLAN.md`.

**What counts as one-way in a data repository**: a decision that puts bytes in
readers' hands under a shape they will code against; a decision about which
repository owns a product, since moving one costs a migration in two places;
and a decision that forecloses an upstream.

## D1 — 2026-10-15 — Its own repository, R2 alone

The MARACOOS HF radar network's currents and waves get a repository of their own (2026-10-15) and **publish to Cloudflare R2 alone**, as every new product since the site pipeline's D13: Pages carries its status alone, and the website's map draws neither. The currents keep the past 24 hours as frames, each hour's finer grids linked inside it.
